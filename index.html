Highlights & Attractions

What You'll Explore Along the Route

From ancient limestone caves to open sea islands, here are the core sights included across our Kilim Geoforest tour packages.

🦇 Bat Cave (Gua Kelawar)

Walk along the wooden footbridge through ancient stalactite caverns home to thousands of native fruit bats.

🦅 Eagle Watching Area

Witness Brahminy Kites and White-bellied Sea Eagles swooping down over the river waters right by the boat.

🐟 Floating Fish Farm

An interactive educational platform showcasing local marine life, stingray feedings, and archerfish displays.

🐊 Crocodile Cave (Gua Buaya)

A natural limestone river tunnel where boats pass through during low and medium tide levels.

🏖️ Coastal Beach Stop

A secluded coastal beach along the Andaman Sea to swim, relax, and unwind (Included in 3H, 4H & Sharing).

🐠 Dangli Island Snorkeling

Enjoy handline fishing or snorkeling with coral reef fish near Dangli Island (Included in 4H & Fun Fishing).

Travel Guide

Tour Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

Important details to know before your Kilim Geoforest Park boat excursion.

Tour Essentials & What to Bring

Attire & Essentials

Comfortable light clothing, sandals or flip-flops, swimwear, and a spare towel.

Sun & Water Protection

Sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, and a waterproof dry bag for phones and cameras.

Small Cash

Cash for Bat Cave entrance fee (RM2 adult / RM1 child) paid at cave gate.

Where does the mangrove boat tour depart from?
All tours depart directly from Kilim River Tourism Jetty, Mukim Kampung Kilim, 07000 Langkawi, Kedah, Malaysia.
What are the differences between 2-hour, 3-hour, and 4-hour tours?
2-Hour (Package A) covers the mangrove river channels, Bat Cave, Crocodile Cave, and eagle watching. 3-Hour (Package B) adds a swimming stop at a coastal beach. 4-Hour (Package C) extends out to Dangli Island for fish feeding and snorkeling.
Are entrance fees to the Bat Cave included?
No. Bat Cave (Gua Kelawar) entry is RM2 per adult and RM1 per child (ages 3–12), collected directly at the cave entrance in cash.
How does the free goggle rental work for Packages B, C, and Fishing?
2 goggles are provided as a complimentary rental for use during your tour. They remain the property of the boat and must be returned to the captain upon trip completion.
Are hotel transfers included in the tour price?
Hotel transfers are not included in base rates. Guests can easily drive or take Grab directly to Kilim Jetty. Return private transfers can be arranged on request via WhatsApp.
