Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions About Kilim Mangrove Tours?

Everything you need to know before booking your Langkawi boat trip directly with our operators.

Where does the Langkawi mangrove tour start?

All our boat tours depart directly from Kilim Geoforest Park Jetty in Langkawi. Exact Google Maps coordinates and location links are shared immediately upon WhatsApp confirmation.

How long is the mangrove tour?

Private boat tours can be chartered for 2 hours, 3 hours, or 4 hours. Shared boat tours run approximately 3.5 hours including lunch.

Is the price per person or per boat?

Private tours are priced per boat for up to the maximum capacity of that vessel (e.g., RM285 flat rate for up to 10 guests). Only the Shared Tour is charged per person (RM80 adult / RM65 child).

How many people can join a private boat?

We offer three private boat sizes: Small Boats (up to 10-12 pax), Medium Boats (up to 15 pax), and Large Boats (up to 20 pax).

Is hotel transfer included in the price?

Hotel transfers are not included in standard boat packages. Most guests travel to Kilim Jetty via Grab or rental vehicle. If you require transfer assistance, feel free to ask us on WhatsApp.

What should I bring on the boat?

We recommend bringing comfortable light clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a camera, and small cash for the Bat Cave entrance fee (RM2 adult / RM1 child).

How do I book and lock in my slot?

Simply click any WhatsApp button on this site to chat directly with our team. State your preferred date, tour type, and estimated guest count to receive instant availability and confirmation details.
