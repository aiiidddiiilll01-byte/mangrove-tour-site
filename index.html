Overview

A Boat Tour Through Langkawi's Living Mangrove Forest

Operating daily straight from Kilim Jetty, our boat excursions offer both personalized private charters and shared join-in trips suited for couples, solo travelers, families, and larger groups. Experience a captivating river journey through Kilim Geoforest Park, where tranquil water highways, ancient mangrove networks, and towering limestone karsts merge into a single stunning eco-sanctuary.

Before cruising out into the open Andaman Sea, your boat will pass through mysterious natural rock tunnels like Gua Buaya (Crocodile Cave) and Gua Kelawar (Bat Cave). You will also get to unwind on quiet coastal beaches, witness local wildlife up close, and enjoy interactive stops at floating fish farms throughout the excursion.

Good to Know

Core Highlights & Terms & Conditions

What makes this tour worth it, and important guidelines to know before you book.

Core Highlights

  • Thriving Wildlife Encounters: Spot inquisitive macaques along the trees, alongside soaring red eagles and white-bellied sea eagles hunting right above the water.
  • Ancient Cave & Karst Explorations: Marvel at natural rock sculptures and millions-of-years-old stalactites as you drift under low-hanging limestone arches into Gua Kelawar (Bat Cave) and Gua Buaya (Crocodile Cave).
  • Interactive Floating Fish Farm: Discover native marine life up close on an authentic ocean-bound platform, observe stingrays, and enjoy optional local seafood dishes.

Terms & Conditions

  • Conservation & Entrance Fees: Gua Kelawar (Bat Cave) entry is not covered in the tour package price; a small cash fee of MYR 2 per adult and MYR 1 per child (ages 3–12) is collected directly at the cave entrance.
  • Booking Confirmation: Every booking includes instant verification. Please notify our support team immediately if you do not receive an official confirmation message.
  • Weather & Water Conditions: Underwater visibility during snorkeling activities (such as near Dangli Island) depends entirely on current weather and sea conditions.
  • Complimentary Child Access: Young children and infants aged 0–2 years are welcome to board and join the tour for free.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Langkawi mangrove tour.

Where does the tour start and depart from?

All tour packages depart directly from Kilim River Tourism Jetty, Mukim Kampung Kilim, 07000 Langkawi, Kedah, Malaysia.

How long is the mangrove tour?

Private tours are available in 2-hour (Package A), 3-hour (Package B), and 4-hour (Package C) durations, each priced by boat size (10/15/20 seater). Shared tours run approximately 3.5 hours including lunch. The Fun Fishing Package runs 4 hours for up to 10 pax.

How do I add lunch to my private tour or Fun Fishing package?

Lunch is available as an optional add-on for any private package (A, B, C or Fun Fishing): Lunch Set A at RM15/pax, or Lunch Set B at RM35/pax (min. 4 pax). Just state your lunch request when contacting us on WhatsApp.

Can I take the rented goggles home after the tour?

No. The 2 goggles provided in the Fun Fishing Package are complimentary rentals for use during the tour only and must be returned to the crew after the tour ends.

Is hotel transfer included in the package?

Hotel transfers are not included in the standard package pricing. Guests usually travel by Grab or rental vehicle to Kilim Jetty. Transfer arrangements can be organized upon request via WhatsApp.

How do I book and pay?

Simply click any WhatsApp button on this page with your preferred date, tour package, and number of guests. Our team will verify availability and share payment details.
