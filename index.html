What to Expect on Your Tour

A seamless experience from booking to stepping off the boat back at the jetty.

01

Meet at Kilim Jetty

Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled departure time at Kilim Geoforest Park Jetty. Free parking is available on site.

02

Cruise the Mangroves

Glide along calm emerald river channels surrounded by vertical limestone karst formations and dense mangrove forests.

03

Caves & Wildlife

Watch sea eagles swoop for food, explore ancient bat caves with stalactites, visit Crocodile Cave, and stop at a floating fish farm.

04

Return & Relax

Finish your 2 to 4-hour adventure safely back at the jetty, or stop for fresh seafood at the floating restaurant.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Langkawi mangrove tour.

Where does the tour start and depart from?

All tour packages depart directly from Kilim Geoforest Park Jetty in Langkawi. Exact Google Maps coordinates and directions will be sent upon booking confirmation.

How long is the mangrove tour?

Private tours are available in 2-hour, 3-hour, and 4-hour durations. Shared tours run approximately 3.5 hours including lunch.

Are prices charged per boat or per person?

Private tours are priced per boat for up to the maximum capacity of that boat (e.g., RM285 per boat for up to 10 guests). Only the Shared Tour is priced per person (RM80 adult / RM65 child).

Is hotel transfer included in the package?

Hotel transfers are not included in the standard package pricing. Guests usually travel by Grab or rental vehicle to Kilim Jetty. Transfer arrangements can be organized upon request via WhatsApp.

How do I book and pay?

Simply click any WhatsApp button on this page with your preferred date, tour duration, and number of guests. Our team will instantly verify availability and share payment/deposit details.
