The Experience

Explore Kilim Geoforest Park by Boat

Kilim Geoforest Park spans over 100 square kilometers of protected mangrove forests, dramatic limestone karst formations, and ancient river channels in northeastern Langkawi.

The Mangrove Ecosystem & Karsts

Navigating through the winding waterways reveals limestone cliffs rising straight from calm emerald waters. The protected mangrove forest serves as a natural sanctuary for wildlife, marine species, and bird life native to Langkawi.

Key Sights: Bat Cave & Eagle Watching

Standard boat tour routes take you to Gua Kelawar (Bat Cave) to view stalactites and roosting bats, past Crocodile Cave, into eagle watching areas where sea eagles gather, and to floating fish farm restaurants along the river.

Shared vs. Private Tour Experience

Shared boat tours provide a structured morning itinerary with set departures and inclusive lunch. Private boat tours offer complete schedule flexibility, custom timing for your party, and dedicated attention from your boat captain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Langkawi mangrove tour.

Where does the Langkawi mangrove tour start?

All our tour packages depart directly from Kilim Geoforest Park Jetty in Langkawi. Exact Google Maps coordinates will be sent upon WhatsApp booking confirmation.

How long is the tour?

Private tours are available in 2-hour, 3-hour, and 4-hour durations. Shared tours run approximately 3.5 hours including lunch.

Is the price per person or per boat?

Private tours are priced per boat for up to the maximum capacity of that vessel (e.g., RM285 per boat for up to 10 guests). Only the Shared Tour is priced per person (RM80 adult / RM65 child).

How many people can join a private boat?

We offer boats for up to 10–12 guests (Small Group), up to 15 guests (Medium Group), and up to 20 guests (Large Group).

Is hotel transfer included?

Hotel transfers are not included in the standard package pricing. Guests usually travel by Grab or rental vehicle to Kilim Jetty. You can contact us on WhatsApp if you require transfer assistance.

What should I bring?

We recommend comfortable light clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a camera, and cash for the Bat Cave entrance fee (RM2 adult / RM1 child).

Can I customise a private tour?

Yes, private tours offer custom itinerary pacing and timing. Feel free to contact our operator team on WhatsApp to discuss specific preferences.

How do I book?

Simply click any WhatsApp button on this site with your preferred date, duration, and guest count. Our team will verify availability and share booking details.
