1. Bookings & Payment (No Deposit, Pay at Jetty)

  • No Deposit Required: No upfront deposit is required to reserve your boat slot via WhatsApp.
  • Pay at Jetty: Full payment in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) is made directly upon arrival at Kilim River Tourism Jetty before boarding.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: Cash, DuitNow QR, Online Bank Transfer, and Credit/Debit Card (Visa & Mastercard).

2. Age Categories & Boat Capacity

  • Adult: Age 13 and above.
  • Child: Age 3 to 12.
  • Infant (Under 3): Free of charge, however infants are still counted as 1 passenger seat for maritime safety and boat capacity limits (10, 15, or 20-seater boats).

3. Inclusions & Exclusions

  • Included: Licensed boatman, boat charter/shared seat, life jackets, and package-specific inclusions (e.g., Set Lunch for Sharing Tour & Fun Fishing; complimentary rental of 2 goggles for Packages B, C, and Fun Fishing).
  • Excluded: Bat Cave (Gua Kelawar) entrance fee (RM2 adult / RM1 child, paid in cash at the cave gate), hotel transfers (available on request at extra cost), and optional lunch add-ons for private tours.

4. Arrival & Punctuality

  • Meeting Point: Kilim River Tourism Jetty, Mukim Kampung Kilim, 07000 Langkawi, Kedah (Operating daily 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
  • Private Tours: Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled departure time.
  • Sharing Tour: Meet by 10:00 AM for the fixed 10:30 AM departure.

5. Weather, Tides & Route Adjustments

  • Kilim Geoforest Park is a natural tidal environment. Our boat captains reserve the right to adjust route sequences or stops based on weather, sea conditions, and tide levels for passenger safety.
  • Passing through Crocodile Cave (Gua Buaya) depends on low-to-medium tide levels. During high tide, the boat will view the cave entrance from outside.
  • Wildlife sightings (eagles, monkeys, and marine life) are natural occurrences and cannot be 100% guaranteed.

6. Safety, Equipment & Personal Belongings

  • Passengers must follow all safety instructions from the boat captain and wear life jackets when directed.
  • Complimentary Goggle Rental: 2 goggles loaned during Packages B, C, and Fun Fishing remain the property of Langkawi Expedition Enterprise and must be returned to the captain upon trip completion.
  • Guests are responsible for their own personal belongings (phones, cameras, wallets). Waterproof dry bags are strongly recommended.
  • We reserve the right to refuse boarding to any guest who is intoxicated or poses a safety risk.
