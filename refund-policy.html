Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Langkawi Expedition Enterprise

At Langkawi Expedition Enterprise, we keep our booking and cancellation process simple and stress-free.

1. Free Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • We offer 100% free cancellation and free time or date changes prior to departure.
  • Because no deposit is required to book, simply inform us via WhatsApp at +60 10-960 6904 as early as possible if your plans change so we can release your boat slot for other travelers.

2. No Refund Once Boarded

  • Once payment is completed at Kilim River Tourism Jetty and passengers have boarded the boat, no refunds will be issued.
  • This includes situations where a guest chooses to end the tour early voluntarily, or if specific natural stops (such as passing inside Crocodile Cave) are inaccessible due to high tide levels.

3. Bad Weather & Safety Cancellations

  • Passenger safety is our highest priority. If our boat captain or local marine authorities determine that sea or weather conditions are unsafe before departure, your tour will be rescheduled to a safer time slot or cancelled at zero charge.
  • Light rain does not automatically cancel a tour, as our charter boats are fitted with overhead canopies and mangrove river channels remain sheltered.
  • If a tour has already departed and must return early due to sudden severe weather for passenger safety, management may offer a partial adjustment or reschedule at its discretion.

4. Contact Us

To cancel, reschedule, or inquire about your booking, please message us directly on WhatsApp at +60 10-960 6904.
