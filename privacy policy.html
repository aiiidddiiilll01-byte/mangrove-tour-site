Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Langkawi Expedition Enterprise

Langkawi Expedition Enterprise (Registration No.: 003893895-M / 2026092400847) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you visit https://www.langkawiexpedition.com/ or book a tour with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire about or reserve a mangrove boat tour, private charter, or fun fishing excursion via our website or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Booking Details: Selected tour package, preferred date and departure time, number of adult/child/infant passengers, optional lunch requests, and hotel transfer details (if requested).
  • Technical Data: Basic website analytics (such as browser type, device type, and pages visited) via Google Analytics and cookies to improve site performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your details solely for your booking and to coordinate your tour, including to:

  • Confirm your boat slot and departure time at Kilim River Tourism Jetty.
  • Contact you via WhatsApp or phone regarding arrival instructions, tide schedules, or weather updates.
  • Arrange optional add-ons such as pre-ordered lunch sets or third-party hotel transfers upon your request.
  • Comply with Malaysian maritime safety and passenger manifest requirements.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Necessary booking details are shared only with our licensed boat captains, jetty crew, floating restaurant partners (for pre-ordered lunch), or local marine authorities if required by law or for passenger safety.

4. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access or misuse. Since bookings require no upfront online card payment, we do not store credit or debit card details on our website. Booking records are retained only as long as needed for operational and accounting purposes.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website links to external services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Facebook, and Instagram. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms.

6. Your Rights & Contact Us

Under Malaysia's PDPA, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us:
