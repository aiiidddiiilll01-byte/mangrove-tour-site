Kilim Mangrove Tour for Groups: Boat Capacity & Price Guide

Planning a private boat tour at Kilim Geoforest Park for a large family, social club, or corporate group? Here is the breakdown on boat sizes, per-person rates, and routes.

Booking a private charter at Kilim Geoforest Park is the most cost-effective and comfortable way to explore Langkawi's mangroves when traveling in a group. Instead of purchasing separate join-in sharing tickets, booking a private vessel reserves the entire boat exclusively for your party, allowing you to choose your departure time and tour pace.

1. Group Boat Capacities & Price Per Person

All private tours depart directly from the official Kilim River Tourism Jetty. Because pricing is charged per boat rather than per person, larger groups enjoy substantially lower costs per head:

Boat Capacity 2-Hour (Pkg A) 3-Hour (Pkg B) 4-Hour (Pkg C) Avg. Cost / Pax
10-Seater Boat RM290 RM340 RM430 ~RM29 – RM43
15-Seater Boat RM360 RM470 RM530 ~RM24 – RM35
20-Seater Boat RM510 RM570 RM730 ~RM25.50 – RM36.50
Group Organizer Tip: If your party exceeds 20 passengers (e.g., 35 or 50 people), we arrange two or three private boats to cruise together in a side-by-side convoy.

2. Selecting the Best Package for Your Group

Option A: 2-Hour Private Tour — Recommended for Multi-Gen Families

If your group includes elderly family members or young children, the 2-Hour Tour (Package A) is typically the best fit. The route remains inside the calm, wave-free mangrove river basin, visiting:

  • Gua Kelawar (Bat Cave) via the easy-to-walk timber boardwalk
  • Interactive Floating Fish Farm and stingray feeding
  • Wild Eagle Watching area (Brahminy Kites and Sea Eagles)
  • Natural limestone archway at Gua Buaya (Crocodile Cave)

Option B: 3-Hour Private Tour — Balanced Pace with Beach Swimming

For groups who want downtime without rushing between stops, the 3-Hour Tour (Package B) adds a swimming stop at a secluded coastal beach along the Andaman Sea. It also includes complimentary rental of 2 goggles.

Option C: 4-Hour Tour — The Complete Snorkeling Circuit

Ideal for active teams, friend groups, and snorkelers. This complete circuit takes your boat beyond the river mouth out to Dangli Island, where hundreds of wild sergeant majors and reef fish gather around the boat for feeding and snorkeling.

3. Group Dining: Pre-Order Lunch on the River

Finding a restaurant in Langkawi that can seat 10 to 20 people without advance notice can cause delays. You can pre-order lunch directly with your boat reservation to be served at the floating platform:

Set Lunch A — RM15 / pax

Fried rice, vegetable soup/tom yum, fresh fruits, and drinking water. Quick, light, and budget-friendly.

Set Lunch B (Seafood) — RM35 / pax

Fresh grilled fish, crispy fried squid, dry chilli chicken, omelette, tom yum, mixed veg, jasmine rice, and iced lemon tea (min. 4 pax).

4. Essential Logistics for Group Organizers

  1. Official Boarding Point: All tours depart directly from Kilim River Tourism Jetty, Mukim Kampung Kilim, 07000 Langkawi.
  2. Ample Free Parking: The jetty provides a large parking compound capable of accommodating multiple rental cars or private tour vans.
  3. Bat Cave Entrance Fees: Admission to Gua Kelawar is RM2 for adults and RM1 for kids (cash only). Collect this in advance to streamline group entry.
  4. Life Jackets: Standard life vests in both adult and child sizes are provided on board and mandatory during transit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can fit on a private Kilim mangrove boat?
We operate 10-seater, 15-seater, and 20-seater charter boats. For groups larger than 20 passengers, multiple boats are assigned to cruise together in convoy.
Is a private boat cheaper than sharing tickets for a group?
Yes. A 2-hour private charter for up to 10 people is RM290 (working out to RM29 per person), which is significantly cheaper than join-in sharing tickets at RM80 per adult.
Can we arrange group lunch at the floating restaurant?
Yes. Groups can pre-order Set Lunch A (RM15/pax) or the Seafood Set B Banquet (RM35/pax, minimum 4 pax), served at the floating fish farm restaurant during the tour.
